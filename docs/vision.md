# Vision and principles

**Core formula:** PEOPLE + PLACE + SIMPLE VERBS + TIME + SPONTANEOUS EVENTS = STORIES

**Core loop:** SEE, APPROACH, TALK, DO, SOMETHING HAPPENS, REMEMBER, RETURN

Players create the stories. The game notices and amplifies them.

## What we are not building

No GPA, energy, status bars, quests, friendship meters, XP, daily streaks, or a complex economy.
Money answers one question: "can I afford this?"

## The seven verbs

Talk, Join, Invite, Follow, Give, Help, Mischief. One verb combines with many people and places,
so a small set of systems produces a large number of situations.

- **Give** transfers something. **Help** is participating.
- **Mischief** should create stories, not resentment: it is opt-out, rate-limited, and never destructive.
- Approach is just movement. Proximity creates opportunity, not obligation.

## The feature test

Before adding anything, it should answer yes to at least one:
helps players meet, communicate, do something together, creates spontaneous situations,
makes the campus feel more alive, or creates something worth remembering tomorrow. Otherwise cut it.

## First milestone

Two real avatars meet, talk, walk somewhere together, and do something together, and it feels natural.
