# Phase 8: Mischief

**Principle:** mischief should create stories, not resentment.

- **Steal a plate / prank** (existing): only between people who both have Mischief on. The toggle is in the bottom bar.
- **Slip out of class:** in a classroom or the Student Hall, the "Slip out" button lets you sneak away (6 s). If a few people escape within 15 seconds, the campus notices.
- **Chase boost:** a robbed player runs 20 percent faster for 8 seconds so the chase is fair.
- **Repeat thief lock:** 3 steals in 10 minutes and you are "watched" (an eyes marker) for 3 minutes and cannot do mischief. It ends by itself.
- Cooldowns and shields stay on all mischief. Opt out any time.
