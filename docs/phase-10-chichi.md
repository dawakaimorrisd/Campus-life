# Phase 10: Chichi (campus buzz)

Chichi is campus gossip. Each player builds a reputation from what they do: helper, connector, funny, trouble. It fades with a half-life of 3 days, so it is never permanent.

- Players are never shown a score. Others see a label ("known for helping people") on the player panel.
- When someone becomes talked about, the campus gets one event: "People are talking about X". At most one per hour.
- Stored in the Postgres `buzz` table.

Chichi only describes. It never gives power, items or rank.
