# Phase 3: Talk

**Goal:** can two players actually communicate naturally?

## Three layers
1. **Nearby talk** (the default): type and press Enter, or tap Say for quick phrases (Yo, Bro, Come, Wait, Where you going?, Let's go, I'm coming, What happened?, 😂). It appears as a bubble above the speaker and fades; nothing becomes a permanent chat window.
2. **Private**: tap a player, Whisper. Deliberate, works at any distance, shown as a notice.
3. **Group talk** is not a separate system: when several people stand together they all hear each other, so a group conversation just happens.

## Physical rules
- Voice carries 10 tiles. Through a wall or doorway between indoors and outdoors it carries 4 tiles and a bit.
- Different floors never hear each other.
- The open-air huts count as outdoors, so conversation flows freely there.

## Expression
Wave, laugh, point, clap, dance (the avatar actually wiggles), no way, hmm. Visible 20 tiles away so a crowd reaction carries.

## Safety
120 character limit, control characters stripped, 6 messages per 8 seconds, with a friendly "Slow down a little" notice.

## Known gaps
No profanity filter, blocking or reporting yet. Add them before a public launch with real students.
