# Roadmap

| Phase | Name | Question it answers | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | World | Can I walk around campus and see other people? | Done |
| 2 | Social presence | Can I naturally approach another player? | Done |
| 3 | Talk | Can two players communicate naturally? | Done |
| 4 | Social verbs | Can two or more players DO things together? | Done |
| 5 | Group behaviour | Does social gravity exist? | Done, awaiting your test |
| 6 | Event engine | Does the campus notice when something interesting happens? | Done, awaiting your test |
| 7 | Money | Does money create choices without becoming the game? | Done, awaiting your test |
| 8 | Mischief | Can players create funny stories? | Done, awaiting your test |
| 9 | Social memory | Does the world remember behaviour? | Done, awaiting your test |
| 10 | Chichi | Does the campus develop its own culture? | Done, awaiting your test |

Not started: real accounts (auth), real photo textures, profanity filter and blocking, upstairs bots. (Sound, time of day and weather now exist in the 3D game.)
Phases 5 to 10 have never been run in a browser yet. Expect adjustments after your first test.
