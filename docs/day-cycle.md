# The campus day

One campus day is about **58 real minutes**. Every part of the day is sized to what people actually do in it, so nothing drags and nothing is over before it starts. The campus clock on screen runs 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM and the chip shows it ("🕗 9:15 AM · Class 2 of 4 · Discussion").

| Real time | Part | Campus time | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 min | Class 1 | 8:00–9:00 | Lesson |
| 90 s | Break | 9:00–9:15 | Walk to the next room |
| 5 min | Class 2 | 9:15–10:15 | Lesson, assignment handed out |
| 90 s | Break | 10:15–10:30 | |
| 10 min | Free time | 10:30–12:00 | Jobs, football, library, friends |
| 5 min | Class 3 | 12:00–1:00 | Lesson |
| 90 s | Break | 1:00–1:15 | |
| 5 min | Class 4 | 1:15–2:15 | Lesson, assignment handed out |
| 90 s | Break | 2:15–2:30 | |
| 12 min | Free time | 2:30–5:00 | Longer afternoon: study, games, jobs |
| 90 s | Dusk | 5:00–6:00 | Head to the Student Center |
| 6 min | Party night | 6:00–9:00 | DJ, dancing, performers |
| 2 min | Bedtime | 9:00–10:00 | Stories and jokes in groups |
| 45 s | Dawn | 7:30–8:00 | Good morning |

## Inside a 5-minute class

| At | Segment | What students do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Roll call | Settle in; attendance is taken |
| 0:15 | Teaching (about 1 min) | Listen; the instructor lectures |
| 1:00 | Quiz 1 | One multiple-choice question |
| 1:24 | Discussion | Talk it through |
| 1:42 | Quick round | Three true/false statements, 12 s each |
| 2:36 | Practice | Work in the room |
| 3:00 | Quiz 2 | One multiple-choice question |
| 3:24 | Review | Wrap the topic |
| 4:00 | Assignments (about 1 min) | Handed out after classes 2 and 4; handed in ones are collected |

About 1 minute teaching, about 3 interactive, about 1 for assignments. Assignments given after class 2 or 4 are due when the next class reaches its wrap-up, so there is always a free period or a long break to do them.

## Switches (testing)

`CLOCK=off`, `CLOCK_SCALE=0.1` (10 times faster), `CLOCK_START=<phase>`, `PARTY=clock|auto|always|off`, `TERM_LESSONS` (classes per term, default 8).
