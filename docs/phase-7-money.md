# Phase 7: Money

**Question:** does money create choices without becoming the game?

Currency is LD. Everyone starts with 100.

| Thing | Cost / pay | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plate of food | 10 LD | Buy at the food counter, then sit to eat |
| Help someone | +15 LD | Paid by the server when the help finishes |
| Music at the Student Center | 15 LD | 90 s cooldown, heard within about 13 tiles |
| Moto ride | 5 LD | 12 s, you move at 2.2x speed |
| Work (shelve books in the Library, stack chairs in the Admin block) | +8 LD | 8 s, 45 s cooldown |

The big button in the bottom right changes to the thing you can do where you stand ("Buy a plate", "Play music", "Shelve books"...). Money is saved in Postgres every 30 seconds and when you leave. There is no shop, no inventory and no leaderboard on purpose.
